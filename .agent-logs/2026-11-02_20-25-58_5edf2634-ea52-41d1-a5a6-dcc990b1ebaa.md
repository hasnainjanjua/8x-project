## 2. Fathom AI Product Workflow Analysis

Based on Fathom AI (`fathom.video`) product research, the 10 core workflows operate as follows and will be faithfully represented in our rebuild:

1. **User Onboarding & Calendar Connection**:
   - Users sign in (or explore via a 1-click Demo Workspace for logged-out evaluators), set their role/department (Sales, Product, Engineering, Leadership, Customer Success), choose their default AI summary template, and connect Google Calendar / Microsoft Outlook with auto-join preferences (e.g., "Record all external meetings", "Record internal meetings").
2. **Meeting Recording & Meeting History ("My Calls" & "Team Calls")**:
   - Meetings are organized into **My Calls**, **Team Calls**, and **Upcoming / Scheduled** calendar events.
   - Because building a live Zoom/Meet WebRTC headless bot is outside practical scope, we will provide an explicit, interactive **"Record / Simulate Live Meeting" & "Upload Audio/Video"** capture drawer that simulates Fathom’s live in-call control bar (live waveform, real-time transcript streaming, one-click live Highlight buttons: *Key Moment*, *Action Item*, *Customer Pain*, *Positive Feedback*) and processes it into a completed meeting.
3. **Recording Playback Alongside Synchronized Transcript**:
   - Split-pane call detail view: Left/Center pane houses the **Video/Audio Player** with custom transport controls (play/pause, `-10s`/`+10s`, playback speed `1x / 1.25x / 1.5x / 2x`, interactive timeline scrubber with color-coded speaker & highlight markers) and the **AI Summary / Action Items / Highlights** tabs; Right pane houses the **Synchronized Chat-Style Transcript**.
   - As the recording plays, the active transcript utterance highlights and auto-scrolls. Clicking any timestamp or transcript line immediately seeks the player to that exact second.
4. **AI Meeting Summaries**:
   - Structured, scannable notes generated immediately post-call: Executive Overview / TL;DR, Key Discussion Points (with inline clickable timestamp badges that jump to that moment in the video), Decisions Reached, and Topics/Chapters breakdown.
   - Includes **"Copy Summary"** (formatted for Slack/Notion/Email/CRM) and **"Ask Fathom"** contextual AI Q&A drawer to query the specific call (e.g., *"What were the main pricing objections?"*).
5. **Switching Summary Templates**:
   - A template selector dropdown on every meeting allows instant regeneration/switching between specialized frameworks:
     - **General / Enhanced Summary** (Default)
     - **Sales Discovery (MEDDPICC / BANT)** (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Pain, Next Steps)
     - **Customer Success / QBR** (Health Score, Wins, Risks, Feature Requests)
     - **1-on-1 Check-in** (Updates, Roadblocks, Career/Feedback, Action Items)
     - **Product Feedback / User Interview** (Current Workflow, Friction Points, Feature Desires, Verbatim Quotes)
     - **Engineering / Sprint Standup** (Progress, Blockers, Architecture Decisions)
6. **Action Items**:
   - Extracted tasks with assigned speaker/owner, due date, meeting source link, timestamp badge (jumping to where the commitment was made), and completion checkbox.
   - Dedicated global **Action Items** view + per-meeting panel, plus a **"Draft Follow-up Email"** modal that formats all decisions and action items by owner ready to send.
7. **Highlighting Moments & Creating Clips**:
   - Users can select any transcript block or click **"Create Highlight / Clip"** at the current playback time, categorize it with a tag (e.g., *Insight*, *Objection*, *Feature Request*, *Decision*, *Praise*), adjust start/end timestamps, and save it as a standalone **Clip**.
8. **Sharing Clips & Public Meeting Views**:
   - Clicking **Share** on a full meeting or an individual clip generates a public shareable URL (`/share/[shareToken]` or `/clips/[clipId]`) with configurable access controls (Include Full Recording vs. Clip Only, Show Summary, Show Action Items).
   - **Logged-out visitors** opening a share link or visiting the deployed app can watch the clip/meeting with synced captions, read the summary, and inspect highlights without hitting an auth wall.
9. **Searching Across Meetings**:
   - Global command palette (`Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`) and dedicated **Search & Explore** page that queries across meeting titles, participant names/emails, company/deal tags, AI summaries, and deep transcript text—returning exact matched transcript snippets with highlighted keywords and direct timestamp jump links.
10. **Handling Long Meetings with Multiple Participants**:
    - Speaker talk-time distribution bar (percentage & duration per participant, monologue detection, question count), chapter timeline bar on the player, and **Filter by Speaker** chips above the transcript so users can isolate a specific stakeholder’s comments in a 60-minute multi-person call.
